# AIO Conversion Metrics

AIO intelligence should connect search visibility to business outcomes.

## AIO Signals

- AI citation fit
- FAQ coverage
- Term definition quality
- Comparison table readiness
- Search intent coverage
- Originality
- Trust signals
- Structured answer readiness

## Conversion Signals

- AIO visits
- Product clicks
- Lead clicks
- Purchases
- CVR
- Revenue
- Saved posts
- Video retention

## Goal

TOMOS should not only rank content. It should show whether AI-search visibility, SNS distribution, and commerce outcomes reinforce each other.
